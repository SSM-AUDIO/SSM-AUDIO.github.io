BEST LIMITER 3

User manual
Version 0.1.0 · VST3 · Windows 10/11 · macOS · Русская версия
1. Install 2. Clipper 3. Limiter 4. Saturator 5. Compare

1. Install

Close your DAW and run the VST3 installer. Reopen your DAW, rescan plugins and insert Best Limiter 3 on a track or bus. Windows requires a 64-bit CPU with AVX2.

2. Clipper

Lower Threshold until clipped peaks appear on the waveform. Use Shape to soften the knee. Delta lets you hear the difference.

3. Limiter

Raise Gain and set Output. Use 1:1 to compare at a matched level.

4. Saturator

Clipper and Limiter are free. Click the Saturator section and enter your purchase email and 500 RUB key. Drive adds saturation and Mix blends it with the original signal. To move your key, choose Deactivate Saturator on this computer in Settings.

5. Compare

Use A/B to compare settings. Click a module title to toggle it or drag to reorder. Bypass switches off the chain.
